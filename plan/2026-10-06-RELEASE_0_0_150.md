## Bug hunt finding
Streamable-HTTP clients POST without a `sessionId`, so their gateway session
is keyed by client id while their GET notification stream has its own UUID.
The SSE forwarder accepts session notifications for either key, otherwise
those clients would stop receiving backend and settings notifications.
